Baazi Games | Newsletters – Research & Copywriting

As part of Baazi Games' newsletter communication, I worked on two editions covering company updates, platform developments, brand initiatives and people within the organisation.

The newsletters brought together updates from different teams and brands, employee stories, product developments and community-focused content. The aim was to turn information from across the organisation into engaging, readable newsletter content.

CONTENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • BalleBaazi's fourth anniversary and reflections from team members.
  • Company and platform updates, including BalleBaazi 3.0.
  • CardBaazi and PokerBaazi updates and initiatives.
  • Team stories, employee introductions and personal interests.
  • Internal updates and engaging features for the newsletter audience.

MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Researching topics and gathering information for newsletter stories.
  • Speaking with relevant people to collect details, perspectives and inputs.
  • Developing and writing the newsletter content from A to Z.
  • Structuring stories, headlines, introductions and individual content sections.
  • Editing and refining copy for clarity, flow and readability.
  • Working with the designer to discuss the content and its visual presentation.

My responsibility covered research, information gathering and complete written content. Visual design and layout were handled by the designer.
