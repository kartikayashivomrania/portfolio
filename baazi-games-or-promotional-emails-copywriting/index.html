Baazi Games | Promotional Emails – Copywriting

Project Overview

As part of Baazi Games' email communication, I worked on the copywriting for two promotional emails designed to share company updates, platform developments, brand communication and community-focused stories with the audience.

The emails covered subjects including BalleBaazi's anniversary, platform updates, developments across Baazi Games' brands, and stories highlighting people and activities within the organisation.

My Contribution

  • End-to-end copywriting for both promotional emails.
  • Writing and structuring all email content, headlines and section descriptions.
  • Organising information into a clear and engaging email flow.
  • Refining messaging for readability, tone and audience engagement.
  • Discussing the creative execution with the designer to communicate the intended content and presentation.

My contribution was focused on writing and content development. The visual design and artwork were handled by the designer.
