Sunstone | Campus Club – Malamaal Quiz Campaign

Malamaal Quiz was a Campus Club promotional campaign by Sunstone, created to engage its student audience through a quiz-based initiative.

I handled the campaign from concept development and communication through creative execution, coordinating the people involved in bringing the promotional mailer to life.

MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Campaign concept development and communication planning.
  • End-to-end copywriting and promotional messaging.
  • Developing the creative brief and guiding the visual direction.
  • Briefing and guiding designers through creative execution.
  • Handling team coordination and overseeing the campaign deliverable.
  • Reviewing the final creative to ensure alignment with the campaign communication.

I was responsible for the campaign's concept, writing, creative direction and team coordination. The visual artwork was executed by the design team under my guidance.
