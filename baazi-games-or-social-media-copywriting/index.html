Baazi Games | Social Media Copywriting

Project Overview

A collection of social media copywriting work created for Baazi Games. The creatives cover cricket-led communication, promotional messaging, topical content and humour-led social media posts.

Social Media Creatives

A selection of five static social media creatives, each using a different copywriting approach — from cricket-themed wordplay and topical references to promotional messaging and humour-led communication.

My Contribution

I was responsible for writing the copy and messaging for all five creatives, adapting the language and communication to suit each post's theme and objective.

My contribution was focused entirely on copywriting. The visual design and artwork were handled by the design team.
