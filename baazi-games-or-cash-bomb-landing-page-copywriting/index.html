Baazi Games | Cash Bomb – Landing Page Copywriting

Cash Bomb was a Baazi Games feature built around match-wise investment leaderboards, cashback and rewards. The landing page explained how the feature worked and helped users understand its participation structure.

I was responsible for writing the landing-page content, translating the supplied product information and mechanics into clear, structured communication for users.

PAGE CONTENT

  • Cash Bomb introduction and feature benefits.
  • Leaderboard structure, prize pools and winning positions.
  • Cash Bomb levels and their relationship with league entry amounts.
  • Eligibility requirements and participation information.
  • Frequently asked questions covering rankings, winnings, cashback and reward distribution.
  • Terms and conditions explaining the feature's rules and limitations.

MY CONTRIBUTION

  • End-to-end landing-page copywriting.
  • Writing and structuring product explanations.
  • Developing clear FAQ responses and terms-and-conditions content.
  • Organising information into a logical page flow for easy understanding.

My responsibility was the writing and content development. I was not involved in the original campaign concept or ideation, and the visual design was handled separately.
