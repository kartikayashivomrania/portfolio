Aatm Nirbhar | Scooty Seekho Cutie – Tutorial Series

Project Overview

Scooty Seekho Cutie is a scooter-learning tutorial series created for Aatm Nirbhar. Designed to make learning to ride a scooty more approachable, the series uses accessible, practical tutorial-style content to help women build confidence and learn a new skill.

My Contribution

  • Series concept and naming — coined "Scooty Seekho Cutie"
  • Content development and tutorial planning
  • Writing and scripting
  • Direction
  • Shooting and footage capture
  • Video editing and post-production
  • Overall end-to-end production
