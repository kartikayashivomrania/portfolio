ABOUT ME

Creative Production. Storytelling. Everything In Between.

I'm Kartikaya Shiv Omrania, a multidisciplinary creative professional with 10+ years of experience across content production, digital media, entertainment and brand campaigns.

I work across the creative process, from developing ideas and writing scripts to video editing, sound design, creative development and production. I enjoy bringing different elements together to turn ideas into compelling content and experiences.

What I Do

Strategy & Creative Direction: Content Strategy · Creative Strategy · Creative Direction · Campaign Development.

Writing & Storytelling: Scriptwriting · Content Writing · Copywriting · Narrative Development · Brand Communication.

Filming & Production: Camera Work · Video Shooting · Shoot Planning · Production Coordination.

Editing & Post-Production: Video Editing · Motion Graphics · Sound Design · Audio Production · Colour Grading.

Campaigns & Digital Media: Campaign Production · Social Media Content · Audience Engagement · Content Development.

Experience & Work

I've worked across television, entertainment, gaming, sports, education and brand marketing, contributing to projects for organisations including Colors TV UK, Baazi Games, Uttarakhand Premier League, Legends League Cricket among others.

My work spans independent productions, brand campaigns and digital content. I've contributed to more than 100 multimedia assets, with individual videos generating over 1 million organic views and campaigns helping grow social channels by 10K+ followers in four months.
