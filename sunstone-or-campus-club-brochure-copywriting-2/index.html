Sunstone | Campus Club – Brochure Copywriting

View Full Brochure (PDF)

Campus Club is a student-focused initiative by Sunstone, designed to help college students access academic support, career opportunities, professional development and community experiences.

I developed the complete written content for the 13-page Campus Club brochure, communicating the initiative's offerings, benefits, programmes and opportunities for students.

BROCHURE CONTENT

  • About Us, Campus Club introduction and vision.
  • Student benefits and exclusive membership offerings.
  • Academic support and educational programmes.
  • Community engagement, events and activities.
  • Career development, placements and internship opportunities.
  • Achievements, placement highlights and student success stories.

MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Complete copywriting for the 13-page brochure.
  • Content development and structuring across all sections.
  • Writing programme descriptions, student benefits and promotional messaging.
  • Developing clear and engaging communication to present the Campus Club offering.

My responsibility was the complete written content and copywriting. The visual design and brochure layout were handled by the design team.
