Baazi Games | T20 Cricket World Cup Anthem

Project Overview

A T20 Cricket World Cup anthem created for Baazi Games. The project involved developing an original rap and musical composition to capture the energy and excitement surrounding T20 cricket.

My Contribution

  • Lyrics and songwriting
  • Music composition
  • Vocal performance
  • Audio mixing
  • Audio mastering

The video visuals were produced by a separate agency. My contribution was focused on the complete audio creation and production.
